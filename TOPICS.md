## Data
- [ ] **Data organization, cleaning & validation in R** — Practical strategies for dealing with messy real-world data: Collect data in a proper way, fix inconsistencies and messyness in R, detecting errors, and validating inputs with `{janitor}` and `{pointblank}`.
- [ ] **Spatial data and maps in R** — Work with geographic data, create maps, and visualize spatial patterns using `{sf}` and `{terra}`.
- [ ] **Research data management and publication** — Make your data FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable): how to document, structure, and publish datasets so they're citable and reusable. (Requested)

## Writing & Communication
- [ ] **Reference management with Zotero** — Organize your literature, cite effortlessly in Word and Quarto, and collaborate on shared libraries.

## R Skills

## Statistics
- [ ] **Linear models in R** — Understand and run linear models in R: interpret coefficients, check assumptions, and report results — no statistics degree required.
- [ ] **Generalized linear models in R** — Extend linear models to count data (Poisson) and binary outcomes (logistic regression), with practical examples from biology and medicine.
